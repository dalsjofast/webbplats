Chemical products for vehicles, cleaning and the home

Under the name DalsjöGrossisten we sell chemical products for vehicles, cleaning and the home. From the one-litre bottle to the IBC tank, to businesses and private customers.

Shop online Quotes for businesses

Our range

Ten product ranges. Our stock range is sold in 1 and 5 litres. Bulk packs and made-to-order products are delivered directly from the supplier to businesses.

Vehicle cleaning

Alkaline wash, cold degreaser, micro-emulsion degreaser, wheel cleaner, foam wash, wax shampoo, oxide remover, concrete remover and insect remover.

AdBlue

In cans from 5 litres, in drums and in 1,000-litre IBC tanks.

Alkylate petrol

For 2-stroke and 4-stroke engines, from 1 litre to 200-litre drums. Collected at our warehouse in Dalsjöfors.

Screen wash

Ready-mixed down to −18 °C, concentrate, summer screen wash and de-icer.

Home and interior

Algae and mould remover, all-purpose cleaner, lighter fluid, lamp oil, stove fuel and toilet chemicals.

Glycol and coolant

Ready-mixed and concentrate for cars, heavy vehicles and OEM requirements, from 1 litre to IBC tank.

Heat transfer fluids

Propylene and ethylene glycol and brine alcohol for heat pumps, cooling and HVAC.

Solvents

Acetone, isopropanol, methylated spirits, white spirit, thinner and cellulose thinner.

Accessories, pumps and tanks

AdBlue pumps, nozzles, hoses, IBC couplings and tanks.

Other fluids

Battery water, antifreeze and fuel line antifreeze.
