03 · Construction

Industrial buildings and self-service car washes, from idea to handover

We plan and build premises for industry, warehousing and workshops, and wash halls where customers wash their own vehicles.

Discuss a project How it works

Two focus areas

Industrial buildings

Hall buildings for production, warehousing and workshops, with doors, clear ceiling height and a layout suited to the business. Built for our own management or for sale.

  • Halls of, for example, 60 × 24 metres with several doors
  • Steel frame with truss beams and clear ceiling height
  • Can be divided into several units with their own doors

Self-service car washes

Self-service washes for cars and light vehicles: wash halls with self-service, proper drainage and oil separators. We can also supply the wash chemicals through DalsjöGrossisten.
