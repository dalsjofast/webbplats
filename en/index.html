Three businesses, one company

Each business stands on its own. Together they cover the whole way from idea and land to operation and consumables.

01 · Trade

Wholesale

Through DalsjöGrossisten we sell chemical products for vehicles, cleaning and the home: alkylate petrol, AdBlue, glycol, screen wash, solvents, degreasers and vehicle wash. At wholesale prices, for businesses and private customers alike.

The whole chain for a self-service car wash

Our three businesses work together. A customer or partner can get help with everything from the building to the chemicals used every day.

  1. 01

    Plan and build

    We design the building or wash hall, with layout, technical systems and building permit.

  2. 02

    Own and manage

    The property is managed for the long term, with maintenance and tenant contact.

  3. 03

    Supply the chemicals

    DalsjöGrossisten supplies wash chemicals, degreasers and consumables at wholesale prices.

A local company in Dalsjöfors

Dalsjöfast AB was founded in 2020 and is based in Dalsjöfors, outside Borås. The company brings trade, property and construction under one roof, with short decision paths and a local presence.
